# Immersive design workflow

Award-level brand experiences: storytelling through scroll, orchestrated motion,
optional 3D, and crafted micro-interactions. The bar is intentional, not busy.

1. **Brief.** Write the full brief from `../SKILL.md`. The Signature line matters most
   here: name the one moment people will remember and derive it from the content.
2. **Research when useful.** Study 3–5 references in the same category with
   `visual-analysis.md` (competitive section) and note patterns to meet and openings to
   differentiate. `ak:ui-ux-pro-max`, when installed, is optional extra input.
3. **Story outline.** List sections as story beats: hook, problem, turn, proof,
   action. For each, write the content, the layout family and the one motion idea.
   Vary layout families where the content differs; keep repeated patterns for
   comparable content.
4. **Plan** in the project's plan location for multi-section builds.
5. **Build static first.** Tokens, layout and typography must look finished with
   motion disabled. Then add motion with `motion-craft.md`: one orchestrated entrance,
   reveals that fit what they reveal, a slow-fast-boom-stop arc for long scroll
   narratives, and reduced-motion alternatives. For 3D, follow `workflow-3d.md`.
6. **Micro-interactions.** Buttons, links, cards, forms and loading states each get a
   designed response (see `aesthetic-recipes.md`, Interaction states).
7. **Assets** via `asset-pipeline.md`; one decisive image beats five average ones.
8. **Critique** with `self-critique-loop.md` at all viewports, with motion on and off,
   and check frame rate on a throttled CPU.
9. **Report**, including what remains unverified. Record durable guidelines in the
   project's design docs when they exist.

Principles: commit fully to the direction; every pixel is deliberate; all elements
belong to one system; one memorable moment is worth more than many small effects;
performance is part of polish.
