# Design quality checks across media

Use for a requested visual artifact. Match the deliverable, audience, content and brand before judging aesthetic quality; reuse an established brief without a mandatory declaration or interview.

## What to verify

- Preserve the user's existing design system or supplied reference when that is the requested route. New art direction may explore alternatives.
- Check legibility, hierarchy, alignment, content fidelity, accessibility and the medium's actual output. A syntax pass alone does not prove rendered quality.
- Use authentic assets and supported claims. Do not invent testimonials, statistics, customer logos or mock screenshots presented as real.
- Avoid decorative content that distracts from the task. Gradients, centered heroes, equal cards, neutral palettes and familiar components are valid when they fit the product or brand.
- Repair demonstrated clipping, collisions, broken states or missing required content. Do not force novelty, motion or visual quotas merely to pass a style score.

## Web-specific resources

`../SKILL.md` owns preserve/replicate/new routing and completion evidence. `aesthetic-recipes.md` contains optional new-design techniques and numeric starting points; `motion-craft.md` applies when motion is selected. Neither recipe mandates seeded variation or an aesthetic ban. User/brand requirements and accessibility govern their use.

For other media, use the producing skill's format and rendering checks. If visual inspection is unavailable, identify that verification gap in the result.
